How it works
1
Click + Add Food to create a new food row. Type the food name in the left field.
2
Enter the macros per 100g — Protein, Carbs, Fat, and Calories — in the grey input fields inside each card. Find these values on the food's nutrition label.
3
Use the slider (or type a number) to set how many grams of that food you ate. The calculated macros update instantly in the row above.
4
Add as many foods as you need. The Total row at the bottom always shows your combined protein, carbs, fat, calories, and total grams.
5
Click ⬇ Download as Excel to export the full log with a totals row as a .xlsx file — named with today's date.
